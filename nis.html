About Freeze-Dried Plasma

Freeze-Dried Plasma (FDP) is a specially processed blood plasma product designed for storage without conventional refrigeration under its approved storage conditions.

It can be reconstituted with the specified sterile diluent according to its approved instructions. It has potential applications in emergency medical care, including military environments.

This website is an inventory demonstration project, not a clinical transfusion or treatment system.
